Pro Tip
Drainage is Critical for Succulents! Always use pots with drainage holes when using this soil mix. The gritty texture allows water to flow through quickly, mimicking the natural desert environment where cacti and succulents thrive. If your plants show signs of stretching or pale color, they may need more light, not more water!

Product Specifications

  • Size Options: 4 qt, 8 qt, 16 qt
  • Ingredients: Sand, perlite, peat moss, composted bark
  • pH Level: 6.0-6.5 (slightly acidic)

  • Organic: Contains organic matter
  • Shelf Life: Indefinite when stored dry
Usage Instructions
  • Use only pots with drainage holes
  • Fill pot halfway with soil mix
  • Place plant in center and fill around roots
  • Leave 1/2 inch space at top for watering
  • Water thoroughly after planting, then sparingly
  • Allow soil to dry completely between waterings
  • Add top dressing of decorative gravel if desired
  • Repot every 2-3 years or when rootbound
