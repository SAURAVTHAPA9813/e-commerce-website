💡 Pro Tip: Clean Cuts = Healthier Plants:

**Always clean your shears** with rubbing alcohol or bleach solution between plants to prevent disease transmission. Sharp, sanitized blades make precise cuts that heal faster, reducing stress on your plants.

🌿 Usage & Maintenance Instructions

  • Proper Cutting Technique: Position blades at a 45-degree angle to the stem. Cut just above a leaf node or bud for optimal regrowth.
  • Blade Capacity: Designed for stems up to 0.75" diameter. Do not force on thicker branches to avoid blade damage.
  • Cleaning: Wipe blades after each use. Disinfect with 70% isopropyl alcohol or 10% bleach solution between plants.
  • Sharpening: Sharpen blades 2-3 times per season using a sharpening stone or professional service for precision.
  • Lubrication: Apply a few drops of machine oil to the pivot bolt monthly to maintain smooth operation.
  • Storage: Store in a dry place with safety lock engaged. Hang or store in a protective sheath to protect blades.
