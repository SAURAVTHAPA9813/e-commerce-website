💡 Pro Tip: Drainage is Essential:

Always use the included drainage saucer to catch excess water. This prevents water damage to furniture while ensuring your plants don't sit in standing water, which can cause root rot.

🌿 Usage & Care Instructions

  • Planting: Add a layer of pebbles or broken pottery at the bottom for improved drainage before adding soil.
  • Drainage: Always place the included saucer beneath the pot to catch excess water and protect surfaces.
  • Watering: Water until moisture drains from the bottom, then empty the saucer after 15-20 minutes.
  • Cleaning: Wipe exterior with a damp cloth. For stubborn stains, use mild soap and water, then dry thoroughly.
  • Indoor/Outdoor Use: Suitable for indoor use year-round. Can be used outdoors in warm seasons, but bring inside before frost.
  • Storage: Store empty pots in a dry, protected area. Avoid extreme temperature changes to prevent cracking.
