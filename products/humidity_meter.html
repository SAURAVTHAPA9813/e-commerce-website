Pro Tip
Test Multiple Spots in Your Pot! Insert the meter probe 2-4 inches deep near the plant roots for accurate readings, not just at the surface. Test several areas of larger pots as moisture distribution can vary. For most houseplants, water when the meter reads in the "dry" zone. Clean the probe after each use to maintain accuracy.

Usage Instructions
  • Insert probe 2-4 inches into soil near plant roots
  • Push probe gently to avoid damaging roots
  • Wait 1 minute for accurate reading to stabilize
  • Check moisture: Red=Dry, Green=Moist, Blue=Wet
  • For pH reading, switch dial to pH mode
  • For light reading, point sensor at light source
  • Clean probe with cloth after each use
  • Do not leave meter in soil permanently
