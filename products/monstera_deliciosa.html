Monstera Deliciosa

(4.5/5 - 127 reviews)

The **Monstera Deliciosa**, also known as the "Swiss Cheese Plant," is an iconic tropical beauty famous for its large, glossy, heart-shaped leaves with dramatic natural splits and holes (fenestrations). Native to the rainforests of Central America, this fast-growing climber brings bold, jungle-inspired elegance to modern interiors and is beloved by designers and plant enthusiasts alike for its striking architectural presence.

💡 Pro Tip: Give It Something to Climb

In the wild, Monsteras climb up trees. Provide a **moss pole or trellis** to support vertical growth, which encourages larger, more fenestrated (split) leaves and healthier development.

🌱 Plant Details & Size

  • Size Range: Medium plants arrive 18"-24" tall in grow pot
  • Botanical Name: Monstera deliciosa (Swiss Cheese Plant, Split-Leaf Philodendron)
  • Growth Rate: Fast-growing with proper light and support
  • Mature Size: Can reach 6-10 feet indoors; much taller outdoors in tropical climates
  • Special Feature: Iconic split and perforated leaves (fenestrations) that develop as the plant matures

🌿 Care Instructions

  • Light: **Bright, indirect light** is ideal. Tolerates medium light but grows slower with smaller leaves.
  • Water: Water when the top 2-3 inches of soil are dry. Prefers consistent moisture but never soggy soil.
  • Humidity: Prefers moderate to high humidity (50-60%+). Mist occasionally or use a pebble tray.
  • Temperature: Thrives in warm temperatures (**65-85°F**). Avoid cold drafts and temperatures below 50°F.
  • Feeding: Feed monthly with balanced liquid fertilizer during the growing season (spring/summer).
  • Pruning: Prune to control size and shape. Wipe leaves regularly to remove dust and enhance photosynthesis.

Plant Requirements

Sunlight

Requires **bright, indirect light** for best growth and leaf fenestration. Tolerates medium light but grows slower.

Watering

Water when the top **2-3 inches of soil** are dry. Prefers consistent moisture but never waterlogged soil.

Pet Safety

NOT pet-friendly. Contains calcium oxalate crystals that are toxic to cats and dogs.

Temperature & Humidity

Thrives in warm temperatures (**65-85°F**) with moderate to high humidity (50-60%+). Avoid cold drafts.
